How it works

Start in the middle. The centre square, The One Offer, is where you pick the one thing you'll sell before your holiday and save it in a New Offer file. Every other square builds on it. Once the offer is live, The Announcement Post moves it into your Live Offers file, ready for the next one. A brand-new business counts: a small reading, a gift card or one download is plenty.

  1. Pick any square, in any order. Tap it, copy the prompt, run it in Claude, and bring back the thing it hands you. Each one is something you make in one sitting.
  2. Make it real. Post it, send it, publish it, or set it up where buyers will use it. The square counts once the thing exists outside the chat.
  3. Share it in the Monthly Challenge category in the community, with the square's name as your title. That is what marks it done. Show what you made and one line on what it's for.
  4. Five in a row is one entry. Any direction, across, down, or corner to corner. The centre square, The One Offer, is done for you, so four of the lines start with a head start.
  5. Fill more rows, get more entries. Twelve lines exist on this board. Every one you complete is another name in the draw.
  6. One thing per square. Each thing you make counts for one square only.
  7. Any holiday works. Every prompt asks which holiday or date you're selling toward, so come back to this board before Valentine's Day, Mother's Day or your business birthday.
