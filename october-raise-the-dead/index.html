How it works

Start in the middle. The centre square, The Graveyard, is where you list everything in your business that went quiet: old posts, retired offers, cold leads, the list you stopped emailing. Dig it up first, then every other square has something to raise.

  1. Pick any square. Tap it, copy the prompt, run it in Claude, and bring back the thing it hands you.
  2. Put it back out. Post it, send it, relaunch it, or lay it to rest. The square counts once the thing is back in front of people, or properly closed.
  3. Share it in the Monthly Challenge category in the community, with the square's name as your title. That is what marks it done. Show what you brought back and one line on what it did.
  4. Five in a row is one entry. Any direction, across, down, or corner to corner. The centre square, The Graveyard, is done for you, so two of the lines start with a head start.
  5. Fill more rows, get more entries. Twelve lines exist on this board. Every one you complete is another name in the draw.
  6. One thing per square. Each thing you bring back counts for one square only, and it has to go back out this month.
