    How the vault works
    The resultWhat changed for your client, in a few words: more bookings, clarity, confidence. The vault sorts by it, and by brand and offer, so you can find proof for whatever you are selling.
    PermissionYes means you can share it. Ask means it came in privately, so ask first; the vault gives you a message for that. Keep private stays in the vault for you.
    The cardAny testimonial becomes a quote card in one tap. Full name, first name or anonymous, light or dark, sized for a post.
    Four prompts for Claude
    1 · Find them

    Dig up your testimonials

    Claude looks through what it can see, asks where the rest are hiding and has you paste them, then hands back an import list sorted by result, with permission marked.

    2 · Proof posts

    Three posts from your strongest

    Built from everything marked Yes. Claude picks the three strongest and writes each as a short proof post, in their exact words.

    3 · Sales page

    Put proof where it sells

    Built from your vault. Claude tells you which quotes belong on which offer's page, and where on the page each one goes.

    4 · Keep them

    Save to your Business Brain

    Built from your whole vault. Claude finds where you keep your business files and asks before it creates or changes anything.

    Keep it safe Your vault lives in this browser. Back it up before you clear site data or switch devices.
