The Resurrection Calendar

Four things that went quiet, back on the calendar.

Dig up the offers, freebies, workshops and series you stopped using. Pick four, give each one a day next month, and leave with a calendar file and a Claude prompt for every one.

These are examples so you can see the shape. Clear them and add your own.
Step 1 · Dig them up

List everything that went quiet: an offer you stopped selling, a freebie nobody sees, a workshop recording, a post series you dropped, a list you stopped writing to. Add them one at a time, or let Claude find them.

Step 2 · Pick four

Choose the four you most want back. A good pick still fits what you sell now and needs a refresh more than a rebuild. Tap to pick, tap again to put it back.

Step 3 · Place them

Give each one its own day and the first small step that gets it moving. One a week keeps each one in front of your people long enough to land.

Step 4 ·

The calendar file opens in Google Calendar, Apple Calendar or Outlook and adds all four days, each with its first step.

Keep it safe Your calendar lives in this browser. Back it up before you clear site data or switch devices.
