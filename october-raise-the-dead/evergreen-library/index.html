    How the shelves work
    RepostStill true, still fits what you sell. Post it again as it is, with a fresh first line.
    RefreshThe idea still works. A date, price, offer, link or old wording needs updating first.
    RestTied to a moment that passed or an offer you closed. It stays on the shelf and out of the pull.
    Three prompts for Claude
    1 · Find them

    Gather your best posts

    Claude looks through what it can see, asks you to paste the rest and share your analytics, then hands back an import list sorted into Repost, Refresh and Rest, with each post marked Hit, Fine or Flop. Paste that list into Import and every post lands on its shelf.

    2 · Refresh a batch

    Update the Refresh shelf

    Built from your Refresh shelf. Claude asks what you sell now, then rewrites each post in your voice with your notes applied.

    3 · Keep them

    Save to your Business Brain

    Built from your whole library. Claude finds where you keep your business files and asks before it creates or changes anything.

    Keep it safe Your library lives in this browser. Back it up before you clear site data or switch devices.
