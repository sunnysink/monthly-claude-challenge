Before You Sit Down

Three minutes to settle your body before a discovery call or a reading. Do it in the car, at your desk, or in the ninety seconds before they join. Work top to bottom.

Nervous before a call is not a sign the call will go badly. It is your body getting ready for something that matters to you. The goal here is not to make the nerves vanish. It is to bring your system down far enough to think clearly and listen well, and that takes about three minutes.

Name what you are carrying, lengthen your breath until your body catches up, put your feet on the floor, and take one steady line in with you.

01

Name what you are carrying

Saying the fear out loud, even to a screen, takes some of its charge out. Type the real one, or tap the closest below.
What are you actually nervous about?
What is happening
02

Lengthen your breath

A longer out-breath is the fastest way to tell your body it is safe. Follow the circle. In as it grows, out as it shrinks. Six rounds is enough.
Ready
Six rounds · about two minutes
Out longer than in. If the pace fights you, let the circle lead and go a little slower.
03

Come back to the room

Thirty seconds. This pulls you out of the call in your head and into the chair you are actually in.
Put both feet flat on the floor and feel the ground hold them
Look up and name three things you can see in the room
Drop your shoulders down, unclench your jaw, let your hands open
Remember one real person you have already helped. Bring them with you
04

Take one line in with you

Not a script. One steady sentence you can come back to if the nerves spike mid-call. Edit it into your own words.
The line you are carrying
OptionalAdd tapping

If you use EFT, this uses the fear you named in step one. Tap lightly, about seven times at each point, and say the line as you go. If tapping is not your thing, skip it. The three steps above stand on their own.

Setup · tap the side of your hand

Say three times: "Even though I'm nervous about this call, I'm allowed to feel this and still show up."

  1. Eyebrow. "This nervous feeling."
  2. Side of the eye. "It showed up because this matters to me."
  3. Under the eye. "I don't have to make it disappear."
  4. Under the nose. "I'm here to understand them, not to perform."
  5. Chin. "I can ask my question and listen."
  6. Collarbone. "My body is allowed to settle now."
  7. Under the arm. "I've helped people before, and I can do it again."
  8. Top of the head. "Steady, and ready to begin."
🕯️ This is yours to keep. It works before a sales call, a reading, a livestream, or any moment you have to show up for someone with something on the line. Come back to it whenever your heart starts going.
