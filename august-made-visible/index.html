How it works
  1. Pick any square. Tap it, copy the prompt, run it in Claude, and make the thing it asks for.
  2. Put it out into the world. Post it, send it, publish it, wherever it belongs. The square counts once the work is visible to somebody besides you.
  3. Share it in the Challenge category in the community, with the square's name as your title. That is what marks it done.
  4. Five in a row is one entry. Any direction, across, down, or corner to corner. The centre square is yours for free, so two of the lines start with a head start.
  5. Fill more rows, get more entries. Twelve lines exist on this board. Every one you complete is another name in the draw.
  6. One piece per square. Each thing you make counts for one square only, and it has to be something you made this month.
$50Cash prize

One winner, drawn on September 1. Every completed line puts your name in once more, so the board rewards finishing a row over collecting scattered squares. Your progress saves on this device, so you can close the tab and pick it back up whenever you have twenty minutes.
