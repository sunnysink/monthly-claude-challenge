Questions, answered
§10FAQ. Real questions people ask before buying (who it's for, time, what's included, payment). Warm and helpful. Do not plant objections they haven't thought of.
Who is this for?

A warm, specific answer that helps the right person recognize themselves.

How much time does each week take?

The honest number, framed so the time reads as going straight into the outcome they want.

What exactly do I get?

List the pieces plainly, the way someone would want them summarized before buying.

What if I fall behind or miss a session?

Reassure them about recordings and pace, so a busy week never feels like a lost one.

Do I need any particular background?

Set the bar honestly, so beginners and the experienced both know where they stand.

When does it start, and can I pay monthly?

Give the start details and point to the payment plan above.
